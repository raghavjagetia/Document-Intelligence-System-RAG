# Code of Conduct and POSH Policy (POL-HR-005)

## 1. Introduction

The Code of Conduct (policy POL-HR-005, version 5.0, effective 1 February 2026) describes the standards of behaviour that Brightpath Consulting LLP expects of every employee, director, intern and contractor. It also incorporates the company's policy on the Prevention of Sexual Harassment at the workplace, framed under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. Every employee must read and electronically acknowledge this Code within 15 days of joining and again every year.

## 2. Integrity and Fair Dealing

Employees must deal honestly with customers, suppliers, competitors and colleagues. Confidential information belonging to customers must only be used for the purpose for which it was shared. Company assets, including laptops, software licences and customer data, must be used only for legitimate business purposes.

## 3. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from business partners only if the value does not exceed ₹2,000 per occasion and the gift is not cash or a cash equivalent such as vouchers. Any gift above ₹2,000 must be declared to the Ethics Office at ethics@brightpath.example within 5 working days and will either be returned or donated to charity. Gifts must never be solicited, and no gift of any value may be accepted from a vendor during an active tender.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must disclose any financial interest in a customer, supplier or competitor.
- Relatives may not be placed in a direct reporting line.
- Outside employment or paid directorships need prior written approval from the Head - People Operations.
- An annual conflict-of-interest declaration must be submitted in BrightpathHR by 30 April each year.

## 5. Speak-Up and Whistleblower Protection

Concerns about fraud, bribery, data misuse or any breach of this Code may be raised with a manager, the Ethics Office, or anonymously through the independent Speak-Up hotline on 1800-425-7788, available 24 hours a day in English, Hindi and Kannada. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a serious violation of the Code.

## 6. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

Brightpath has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. Unwelcome physical contact, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography, demands for sexual favours and any other unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature, whether in the office, at off-sites, on business travel or in online channels, are prohibited.

### 6.1 Internal Committee

The Internal Committee (IC) has 4 members. It is chaired by the Presiding Officer, Dr. Meera Krishnan, Director - Engineering, and includes an external member from the Sakhi Suraksha Foundation, a non-governmental organisation working on women's rights. At least half the members of the IC are women.

### 6.2 Complaint and Inquiry Process

A written complaint may be submitted to the IC at ic@brightpath.example within 3 months of the incident, extendable by a further 3 months for valid reasons. The IC will offer conciliation if the complainant requests it, and will otherwise complete its inquiry within 90 days of receiving the complaint. The IC's report and recommendations are submitted to management within 10 days of completing the inquiry, and management must act on them within 60 days.

### 6.3 Awareness

POSH awareness training is mandatory for all employees every year and must be completed by 31 July. IC members receive an additional annual workshop conducted by an external expert.

## 7. Consequences of Violation

Violations of the Code may result in counselling, a written warning, suspension, withholding of increment, or termination of employment, depending on severity. Where a violation is also a criminal offence, the company will cooperate fully with law enforcement authorities.
