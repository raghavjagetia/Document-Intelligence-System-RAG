New Employee Onboarding Guide 2026

Welcome to Orion

Welcome to Orion Softworks Pvt. Ltd. This guide walks you through everything that happens between accepting your offer and completing your first 90 days. Your onboarding is coordinated by the People Operations team through OrionStart, our onboarding portal, where you will find your checklist, documents to upload and the contact details of your onboarding buddy.

Before Day One

  • Upload your PAN, Aadhaar, previous employer relieving letter and the last three salary slips to OrionStart.
  • Complete the background verification consent form; verification is carried out by VeriTrust Screening and is normally completed within 15 days.
  • Share your bank account details for salary credit and your existing Universal Account Number (UAN) for Provident Fund transfer.
  • Choose your laptop keyboard layout and preferred operating system (Windows or Ubuntu) in the IT request form.

Your First Day

Please report at 9:30 AM to the Tower B reception, Lakeview Tech Park, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru 560103. Carry one original photo identity document. Your temporary access card will be issued at reception, and your permanent photo ID card will be ready within 2 working days. The IT service desk will hand over a Dell Latitude 7440 laptop pre-configured with disk encryption, the endpoint protection agent and the corporate VPN client.

Mandatory Trainings

Three e-learning modules must be completed within your first 30 days: Information Security Essentials, Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH), and the Code of Conduct. Completion is tracked in OrionLearn, and access to production systems is granted only after the Information Security module is passed with a score of at least 80%.

Your Onboarding Buddy

Every new joiner is paired with a buddy from a neighbouring team who has been with Orion for at least one year. Your buddy will meet you on Day 1, take you to lunch in the first week, and check in fortnightly for the first three months. Buddies are recognised with a ₹3,000 gift voucher for each successful onboarding.

Probation and Confirmation

All new employees serve a probation period of 6 months. A 30-60-90 day plan is agreed with your manager during the first week, and a formal probation review is held at the end of month 5. On successful review you will receive a confirmation letter; probation may be extended once by up to 3 months. The notice period during probation is 15 days, rising to 60 days after confirmation.

Payroll and Benefits Enrolment

Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month. Employees who join after the 20th of a month receive their first salary along with the following month's payroll. Enrol yourself and your dependents in the group health insurance plan within 30 days of joining in OrionHR; dependents cannot be added mid-year after this window closes, except on marriage or childbirth.
