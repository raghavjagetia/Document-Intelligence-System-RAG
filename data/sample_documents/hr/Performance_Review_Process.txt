ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW PROCESS (POL-HR-031)
==============================================

1. PURPOSE
----------
Policy POL-HR-031 describes how Aster Logistics Pvt. Ltd. sets goals, reviews performance and links outcomes to pay. The process is designed to be transparent, evidence-based and fair, and to give every employee at least two structured conversations about their growth every year.

2. REVIEW CYCLE
---------------
The performance year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Goals (OKRs) for the year are set in AsterHR by 30 April. A mid-year check-in is held in October, during which goals may be revised if business priorities have changed. The annual review takes place in April of the following year.

  - Self-appraisal window: 1 April to 15 April.
  - Manager assessment: 16 April to 30 April.
  - Calibration committee meetings: first two weeks of May.
  - Rating communication and increment letters: by 15 June, effective from 1 July.

3. 360-DEGREE FEEDBACK
----------------------
Employees in Band L4 and above receive 360-degree feedback from a minimum of 4 reviewers, including at least one peer from another department and, for people managers, at least two direct reports. Feedback is anonymised before it is shared with the employee.

4. RATING SCALE
---------------
  Rating | Label                        | Indicative distribution
  -------+------------------------------+------------------------
  5      | Exceptional                  | Up to 15% of employees 
  4      | Exceeds Expectations         | About 25%              
  3      | Meets Expectations           | About 50%              
  2      | Partially Meets Expectations | About 8%               
  1      | Does Not Meet Expectations   | About 2%               

5. LINK TO COMPENSATION
-----------------------
Annual salary increments are determined by the final calibrated rating and the employee's position within the salary band. An employee rated 5 (Exceptional) receives an increment of between 12% and 15%. An employee rated 4 receives between 8% and 10%. An employee rated 3 receives between 5% and 7%. Employees rated 2 receive a cost-of-living adjustment of 3%, and employees rated 1 are not eligible for an increment. Employees rated 5 in two consecutive cycles are automatically nominated for the promotion committee.

The annual performance bonus is paid in July. The bonus multiplier is 1.5x of target for rating 5, 1.2x for rating 4, 1.0x for rating 3 and zero for ratings 1 and 2.

6. CALIBRATION
--------------
Calibration committees are chaired by the department head and include the HR Business Partner and at least two managers from other teams. The committee compares evidence across teams to ensure that ratings mean the same thing everywhere in the company and that the distribution guidance is respected.

7. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN
-------------------------------
Employees rated 1, or rated 2 in two consecutive cycles, are placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP lasts 60 days and sets out no more than five specific, measurable objectives. The employee meets the manager and HR Business Partner every two weeks during the PIP. At the end of the 60 days the PIP is closed as successful, extended once by 30 days, or the employee may be separated with notice in accordance with the employment contract.

8. APPEALS
----------
An employee who disagrees with their final rating may file an appeal in AsterHR within 10 working days of the rating being communicated. Appeals are reviewed by a panel consisting of the skip-level manager and a senior HR leader, and a decision is communicated within 21 days.
