Our New Whitefield Office Opens on 2 November

Our second Bengaluru office, on the 3rd floor of Greenfield Enclave on ITPL Main Road, Whitefield, opens on 2 November 2026. The 12,500 sq ft space has 220 workstations, 14 meeting rooms, a podcast studio and a wellness room. The Cloud Operations and Data Science teams will move first. A shuttle will run every 30 minutes between the Bellandur and Whitefield offices from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

HackOrion 2026: And the Winner Is...

Forty-two teams took part in HackOrion 2026, our annual 36-hour hackathon, held on 19 and 20 September. The winning team, ByteBrigade, built a voice-based ticket logging assistant in Kannada and Hindi for field technicians, and took home the first prize of ₹2,50,000. The runner-up, Team QueueBusters, built a tool that predicts SLA breaches from ticket text, and the People's Choice award went to Team GreenOps for a cloud carbon dashboard.

  • Winner: ByteBrigade - ₹2,50,000
  • Runner-up: QueueBusters - ₹1,25,000
  • People's Choice: GreenOps - ₹50,000

CSR: 5,000 Saplings Planted

Over three weekends in August, 380 employee volunteers planted 5,000 native saplings near Hesaraghatta lake in partnership with a local environmental trust. Volunteers will return in February to check on survival rates.
