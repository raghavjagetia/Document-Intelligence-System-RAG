# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (IPA-2026-012)

## 1. Parties

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (reference IPA-2026-012) is made on 21 May 2026 between Kiran Joshi, an independent software consultant residing at Kothrud, Pune 411038 (the "Assignor"), and Helix Systems Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru (the "Assignee").

## 2. Background

Between 1 December 2025 and 30 April 2026 the Assignor developed, under a consultancy engagement with the Assignee, a mobile software development kit code-named Project Sentinel, which enables offline ticket capture and secure sync for the HelixDesk field-service app on Android and iOS. The parties wish to record the transfer of all rights in that work to the Assignee.

## 3. Assignment

In consideration of a lump-sum payment of ₹3,20,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Twenty Thousand only), the receipt of which the Assignor acknowledges, the Assignor irrevocably assigns to the Assignee, for the entire world and for the full term of protection, all right, title and interest in the Assigned Works, including copyright in source code, object code, documentation, designs and test suites, and any inventions, improvements or know-how conceived in the course of the engagement.

The assignment is made under Section 18 of the Copyright Act, 1957. The parties expressly agree that the assignment shall not lapse if the Assignee does not exercise the rights within one year, and that the territorial extent of the assignment is worldwide.

## 4. Assigned Works

- The Project Sentinel SDK source repository as at commit tag v1.4.0.
- The offline encryption module using SQLCipher-compatible storage.
- API documentation, architecture diagrams and the automated test suite of 1,240 test cases.
- All interim builds delivered between December 2025 and April 2026.

## 5. Excluded Prior Inventions

The Assignor's pre-existing open-source charting library "OpenGrid", released under the MIT licence in 2022, is excluded from this assignment. The Assignor grants the Assignee a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use OpenGrid to the extent it is incorporated into the Assigned Works.

## 6. Moral Rights

To the extent permitted by law, the Assignor waives the right to be identified as the author of the Assigned Works and agrees not to object to any modification of them.

## 7. Further Assurance

The Assignor shall sign any further documents reasonably required to register or enforce the Assignee's rights, including patent applications, for 3 years after the date of this Agreement, at the Assignee's cost.

## 8. Warranties

- The Assigned Works are original and do not infringe any third-party rights.
- No part of the Assigned Works has been licensed or assigned to any other person.
- No open-source component under a copyleft licence such as GPL has been included.

## 9. Non-Solicitation and Confidentiality

For 12 months from the date of this Agreement the Assignor shall not solicit any customer of the Assignee for which the Assignor performed work. The Assignor shall keep confidential all non-public information about the Assignee's products indefinitely.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and disputes are subject to arbitration seated in Bengaluru. Signed by Kiran Joshi and by Arjun Bhatt, Chief Technology Officer, for the Assignee.
