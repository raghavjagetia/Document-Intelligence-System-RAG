Helix Systems Pvt. Ltd.

Service Level Agreement SLA-2026-118

1. Scope

This Service Level Agreement (SLA-2026-118) forms Schedule B to the Master Services Agreement CNT-2026-9941 between Helix Systems Pvt. Ltd. and Orchid Retail Ventures Pvt. Ltd. It defines the service levels for the hosted store systems, the e-commerce order platform and the IT service desk, how performance is measured, and the service credits payable when targets are missed.

2. Availability Commitment

The Service Provider commits to a monthly availability of 99.9% for production systems, measured from synthetic transactions executed every minute from two independent locations. Availability is calculated as total minutes in the month minus minutes of unplanned downtime, divided by total minutes in the month. Planned maintenance is excluded.

3. Maintenance Windows

Planned maintenance is performed on Sundays between 01:00 and 05:00 IST. The Client must be notified at least 72 hours in advance. No planned maintenance may be scheduled during the Client's blackout periods: the 10 days before Deepavali and the period from 20 December to 2 January.

5. Service Credits

If monthly availability falls below the commitment, the Client is entitled to a service credit calculated as a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected month:

Monthly availabilityService credit
99.0% to below 99.9%5% of monthly fee
95.0% to below 99.0%10% of monthly fee
Below 95.0%20% of monthly fee

Service credits are capped at 25% of the monthly fee in any month and are applied against the next invoice. Service credits are the Client's sole financial remedy for availability shortfalls, except where availability is below 95.0% for three consecutive months, in which case the Client may terminate the Agreement for cause without paying any exit fee.

6. Reporting and Governance

  • A monthly service report is delivered by the 5th working day of the following month.
  • A root-cause analysis is delivered within 5 business days of every P1 incident.
  • Quarterly service reviews are held between the Engagement Manager and the Client CIO.
  • Customer satisfaction (CSAT) on closed tickets must remain at or above 4.2 out of 5.

7. Exclusions

Service levels do not apply to downtime caused by force majeure events, Client-requested changes executed against the Service Provider's advice, failures of Client-owned network links in stores, or third-party payment gateway outages.
