# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Horizon Health Tech Pvt. Ltd. and Deltawave Telecom

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 17 March 2026 between Horizon Health Tech Pvt. Ltd. and Deltawave Telecom.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 5 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 23,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Pune have exclusive jurisdiction.
