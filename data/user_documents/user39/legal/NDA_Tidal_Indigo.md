# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Tidal Energy Pvt. Ltd. and Indigo Retail

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 24 February 2026 between Tidal Energy Pvt. Ltd. and Indigo Retail.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 5 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 11,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Chennai have exclusive jurisdiction.
