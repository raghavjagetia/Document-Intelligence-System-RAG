# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Juniper Fintech Pvt. Ltd. and Orchid Hotels

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 15 May 2026 between Juniper Fintech Pvt. Ltd. and Orchid Hotels.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 3 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 18,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Hyderabad have exclusive jurisdiction.
