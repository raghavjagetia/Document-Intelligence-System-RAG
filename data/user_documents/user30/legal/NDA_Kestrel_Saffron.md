# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Kestrel Aerospace Pvt. Ltd. and Saffron Foods

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 15 July 2026 between Kestrel Aerospace Pvt. Ltd. and Saffron Foods.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 3 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 15,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
