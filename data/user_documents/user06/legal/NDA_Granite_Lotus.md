# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Granite Infra Pvt. Ltd. and Lotus Textiles

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 11 July 2026 between Granite Infra Pvt. Ltd. and Lotus Textiles.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 5 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 31,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Mumbai have exclusive jurisdiction.
