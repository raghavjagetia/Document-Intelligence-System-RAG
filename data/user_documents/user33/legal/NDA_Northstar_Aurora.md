# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Northstar Pharma Pvt. Ltd. and Aurora Analytics

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 3 January 2026 between Northstar Pharma Pvt. Ltd. and Aurora Analytics.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 2 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 6,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Hyderabad have exclusive jurisdiction.
