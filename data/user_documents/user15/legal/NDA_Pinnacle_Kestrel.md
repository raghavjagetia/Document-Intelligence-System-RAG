# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Pinnacle Motors Pvt. Ltd. and Kestrel Aerospace

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 19 April 2026 between Pinnacle Motors Pvt. Ltd. and Kestrel Aerospace.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 2 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 11,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Kolkata have exclusive jurisdiction.
