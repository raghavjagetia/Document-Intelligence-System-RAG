# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Monsoon Media Pvt. Ltd. and Deltawave Telecom

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 24 March 2026 between Monsoon Media Pvt. Ltd. and Deltawave Telecom.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 3 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 9,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.
