# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Cedarwood Foods Pvt. Ltd. and Redwood Realty

## Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 13 February 2026 between Cedarwood Foods Pvt. Ltd. and Redwood Realty.

## Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret for 3 years from the date of disclosure and use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship.

## Remedies

Breach entitles the disclosing party to injunctive relief and damages of up to Rs. 41,00,000.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law; courts at Pune have exclusive jurisdiction.
